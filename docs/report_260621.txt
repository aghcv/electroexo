## Main Results

- The Layer 3 comparison now produces distinct Ca2+, ROS, ATP, mitochondrial, osmotic, damage, and viability responses across baseline, high-dose, calcium-limited, and slow-osmotic-recovery cases.
- In the current example outputs, wide-pulse/high-dose conditions increase peak cytosolic Ca2+, mitochondrial Ca2+, ROS, mitochondrial depolarization, damage, and EV release while reducing viability relative to baseline.
- Calcium-limited medium reduces peak cytosolic Ca2+, mitochondrial stress, ROS, damage, and improves viability relative to baseline.
- Slow osmotic recovery leaves peak Ca2+ similar to baseline but increases osmotic stress and terminal damage.
- The Layer 4 remodeling/repair comparison now resolves PS exposure, calpain, annexin, repair state, actin disruption, resealing, and repair-associated shedding.
- Calpain inhibition reduces calpain activity and actin disruption while increasing the repair-state proxy.
- Strong annexin/lysosomal support increases the repair-state proxy and repair-associated shedding.

## Current Limitations

- The framework is structurally broader than the June 17 version, but it is still not experimentally validated.
- Many equations remain reduced closures rather than detailed electrodiffusion, channel, mitochondrial, cytoskeletal, or EV-biogenesis models.
- Calibration targets have been identified, but most parameter values remain provisional.
- Scenario outputs are now more differentiated than before, but EV yield, subtype balance, cargo, potency, and quality-control predictions still need fitting against project-specific in vitro data.
- The Typer/Python 3.13 CLI help issue remains a practical usability gap.
