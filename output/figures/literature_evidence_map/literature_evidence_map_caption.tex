\caption{Literature support and curation coverage for the modular electro-exocytosis framework. Each spoke represents one of 95 submodule records in the supplied literature tracker (47 biological/process evidence records and 48 computational-framework records), rather than a distinct publication. Spokes retain workbook order and are grouped into the eight modules defined in the current manuscript and paired supporting tables. The numeric spoke identifiers are linked to table, submodule, source row, and full citation strings in the accompanying source-data file. From the center outward, tracks show record role, the recorded mechanistic-confidence category, five citation slots, curation status as recorded, and module identity. Confidence is assigned only to biological/process records; gray computational cells mean that no category is assigned in this field. Citation slots follow Paper 1 to Paper 5 from the center outward and indicate populated references, not citation rank, independent evidence, study quality, or support strength. Repeated citations across records are retained. The sheet contains 423 listed citation entries and one invalid placeholder ('s', cell N52, spoke 51), which is shown separately and excluded from citation counts. Open diamonds mark four records without listed citations (spokes 11-14: membrane polarization, plasma-membrane nanopores, organelle electroporation, and permeability recovery). These remain marked Complete in the source. Curation status describes workbook metadata and does not establish model implementation, calibration, or validation. Cell-state/disease modifiers are cross-cutting in the manuscript and have no separate tracker records, so they are not represented as a ninth sector. The size-composition-observation bridge described in the current supplement also has no separate tracker sector. The map summarizes the supplied curation, without independent bibliographic verification or study-level risk-of-bias appraisal. EV, extracellular vesicle; Ca$^{2+}$, calcium ion; ROS, reactive oxygen species; ATP, adenosine triphosphate; QC, quality control.}
