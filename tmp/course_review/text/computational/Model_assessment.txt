✓ Assessment of model fit (how well does the model fit the data), and least 
square estimates of model parameters: 
 
1. A measure of how well does the model fit the data.  
o Involves determining whether the residuals (model fit  data) are 
consistent with the stochastic part () of the regression model (using 
runs test).  
o Other global measures of consistency between model fit and data 
include coefficient of variations, coefficient of determination (linear 
regression). 
 
2. Measures of confidence in the least square estimates of the model 
parameters 
o Confidence intervals 
o Correlation matrix 
o Sensitivity analysis 
 
3.  A measure of how well does the model compare to other models 
(when applicable), or when you have different candidate models: 
o F-test 
o Akaike information criterion 
o Schwarz criterion
