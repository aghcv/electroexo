Brief Review of Ordinary Differential Equations (ODEs)  
 
 An ODE is any statement linking the values of a dependent (or state) variable 
to its derivative(s) with respect to a single independent variable (e.g., time).

in the equation.

 Boundary conditions: To obtain a unique solution of an nth order 
ODE (or of a set of “n” simultaneous first-order ODEs), it is necessary 
to specify “n” values of the dependent variables (or their derivatives) at 
specific values of the independent variable. Those are referred to as the 
initial or boundary conditions. 
 
 Initial-value vs. boundary-value problem 
 
o Initial-value problem: Values of dependent variables and/or their 
derivatives are all known at the initial value of the independent 
variable (normally t = 0 in the time domain or x = 0 in the space 
domain).
