 Law of Mass Action 
o Fundamental law of a chemical reaction 
o Describes the rate at which chemicals, whether macromolecules or simple 
ions, collide and interact to form different chemical combinations. 
o According to the law of mass action: 
 When two or more reactants are involved in a reaction step, the rate (or 
velocity) of the reaction is proportional to the product of their 
concentrations (in units of Molar) raised to the power of their 
stoichiometric coefficients.  
o Assumptions include constant temperature and pressure.
