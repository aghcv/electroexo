Cell membrane distinguishes one cell from the next.
Cell membranes do the following:
a) Regulates exchange of salts, nutrients, and waste with the environment.
b) Mediate communication between the cytosol and the environment.
c) Maintain cell volume, shape, and integrity.

Example: Channels and Transporters Across the Cardiac Cell 
Membrane Governing Excitation-Contraction Coupling
 Leaky pores (e.g. background Na+, K+, Ca2+ and Cl- channels) and ATPase pumps (e.g. Ca2+
ATPase and 3Na+/2K+ ATPase) govern the resting membrane potential in cardiac myocytes.
 V oltage-gated channels (e.g. Na+, K+ and Ca2+ channels), ligand-gated channels (e.g. RyR, 
IP3R, KCa, KATP, and P2Y7), and other ion exchangers govern the cell excitation-contraction 
coupling and many other functions defining the cell physiology and pathophysiology.

Markovian Modeling of Ion Channel Kinetics 
 In probability theory, a Markov model is a stochastic model used 
to model randomly changing systems, where it is assumed that 
future states depend only on the present state and not on the 
sequence of events that preceded it. 
 Ion channel kinetics (opening and gating) is highly stochastic. So 
recently there have been a significant interests in modeling ion 
channels kinetics using Markovian state modeling, rather than 
Hodgkin-Huxley formalism (phenomenological).
A. Clancy & Rudy (2002) INa
B. Jafri et al. (1998) ILCC

===== PAGE 21 =====
Characteristics of L-Type Ca2+ Channel 
 L-type Ca2+ channels (LCC) are special kind of voltage-gated Ca2+
channels. LCC are closed in a resting cell (V = -90 mV)
 LCC opens when membrane depolarizes that leads to Ca2+ entry 
into the cell and Ca2+ release from SR via RyRs
 LCC kinetics (opening and gating) is highly complex. It is both 
activated and inactivated by voltage and inactivated by Ca2+ in 
the dyadic space (localized between T-tubules and SR)
Rose et al. (1992)

(Faber et al, 2007)
 Two modes: Mode V and Mode Ca
 Each mode goes through inactivation
 Rate constants of transitions are V-dependent and Ca-dependent
 Kinetics of the LCC is modeled via a system of ODEs for different states
 Model is parameterized to describe the well-known features of the LCC
