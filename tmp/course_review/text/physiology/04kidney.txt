===== PAGE 34 =====
Aldosterone vs. ADH
Volume depletion  Aldosterone
• Loss of water and electrolytes
• Vomiting, hemorrhage, diarrhea, burns
1. Drop in blood pressure
2. Aldosterone release
3. Na+ reabsorbed and water by osmosis
Dehydration  ADH
• Loss of water but not electrolytes
• Lack of water, excess sweating, diabetes
1. Tissue fluids become hyperosmotic
2. Osmoreceptors stimulated
3. ADH release corrects osmotic imbalance
