===== PAGE 9 =====
The Endocrine Glands
The major hormone producing glands and organs are the:
1. HYPOTHALAMUS (ADH, oxytocin, releasing, inhibiting hormones)
2. PITUITARY GLAND
a. Anterior (stimulating hormones)
b. Posterior (storage of ADH and oxytocin)
3. THYROID GLAND (thyroxine, triiodothyronine, calcitonin)
4. PARATHYROID GLANDS (parathyroid hormone)
5. ADRENAL GLANDS
a. Cortex (aldosterone, cortisol, androgen)
b. Medulla (epinephrine, norepinephrine)
6. PANCREAS (insulin, glucagon)
7. OVARIES (estrogen and progesterone)
8. TESTES (testosterone)
9.STOMACH (gastrin, ghrelin)
10. DUODENUM (cholecystokinin, secretin)
11. KIDNEY and LIVER (erythropoietin and somatomedin)
12. PLACENTA (estrogens and progesterone)
13. TROPHOBLAST (human chorionic gonadotropin)

Adrenal Cortex
Cushing’s Syndrome (hyperfunction)
• Excess cortisol  excess gluconeogenesis
• Loss of protein- muscle wasting, bruising, poor 
would healing, thinning of the skin, 
osteoporosis
• Hyperglycemia  Diabetes mellitus
• Excess aldosterone  excess Na+ and water 
reabsorption  hypertension
Addison’s Disease (hypofunction)
• Low aldosterone  hypotension  death
• Low cortisol  hypoglycemia  lethargy

===== PAGE 51 =====
Pancreatic Hormones
Insulin (beta cells)
• “Meal-time hormone”
• Increases glucose uptake in liver, muscle, fat
– glycogenesis in liver and muscle (glycogen formation)
– lipogenesis in fat (fat deposition)
Glucagon (alpha cells)
• “Between-meal hormone”
– glycogenolysis in liver (glycogen breakdown)

===== PAGE 52 =====
Diabetes Mellitus
• Hyperglycemia- high blood sugar
• Glycosuria- glucose in the urine
• Polydypsia- increased urine output (hyperosmotic 
urine)
• Ketosis- muscle reliance on fats for energy results 
in elevated blood levels of ketone bodies.
• Acidosis- ketone bodies are acidic.
• Coma- acidosis suppresses the nervous system

===== PAGE 53 =====
Insulin Shock
• Overdose of insulin
• Hypoglycemia as insulin causes increased uptake 
by muscle and liver
• Neural suppression- the nervous system relies 
solely on glucose for energy
• Subject loses consciousness
