===== PAGE 19 =====
Surfactant
Alveoli are air-filled but coated with liquid (requirement for 
living cells). Therefore, there is surface tension.
Alveoli are coated with a surfactant (dipalmitoyl lecithin) 
to reduce surface tension.
From the previous figure:
• Lung compliance in air-filled lungs is 200 ml/ cm H2O 
• Lung compliance in saline-filled lungs is about 600 ml/ cm H2O
• Therefore, about 2/3 of the compliance of an air-filled lung is due to
surface tension, even with surfactant.
• And only about 1/3 of the compliance is due to the properties of
lung elastic tissue.

===== PAGE 20 =====
Surfactant
Surface tension is related to pressure and radius by the 
Law of Laplace: 
But, assuming not all alveoli are the same radius, if 
surface tension were constant:
• Alveoli of small radius would have high pressure
• Alveoli of large radius would have low pressure
• Small alveoli would therefore empty in large ones.
Besides reducing surface tension, surfactant also allows 
surface tension to change appropriately.

===== PAGE 25 =====
Ventilation
Resting Lung Volumes:
• Tidal volume = 500 ml/breath
• Dead space volume = 150 m/breath
• Alveolar volume = (500 – 150) = 350 ml/breath
Ventilation = volume  frequency
If breathing frequency = 12 breaths/min, then
Minute ventilation = 500 ml/breath  12 breaths/min
= 6.0 liters/min
Dead space ventilation = 150 ml/br  12 br/min
= 1.8 liters/min
Alveolar ventilation = 350 ml/br  12 br/min
= 4.2 liters/min

===== PAGE 26 =====
Obstructive Lung Disease
Obstructive Impairment: Elevated airway resistance
Examples:
• Asthma (airway smooth muscle)
• Bronchitis (over-production of mucus)
• Emphysema (loss of elastic recoil)
Compensation: Increase tidal volume, decrease frequency
Minute ventilation = 675 ml/breath  8 breaths/min
= 5.4 liters/min
Dead space ventilation = 150 ml/br  8 br/min
= 1.2 liters/min ( by 0.6 liters/min)
Alveolar ventilation = 525 ml/br  8 br/min
= 4.2 liters/min (normal value)

===== PAGE 28 =====
Restrictive Lung Disease
Restrictive Impairment: Decreased Lung Compliance
Examples:
• Pulmonary fibrosis (elastic tissue)
• Infant respiratory distress syndrome (surface tension)
Compensation: Decrease tidal volume, increase frequency
Minute ventilation = 360 ml/breath  20 breaths/min
= 7.2 liters/min
Dead space ventilation = 150 ml/br  20 br/min
= 3.0 liters/min ( by 1.8 liters/min)
Alveolar ventilation = 210 ml/br  20 br/min
= 4.2 liters/min (normal value)

a. Located in arch of aorta and carotid

c.  Respond quickly to changes
