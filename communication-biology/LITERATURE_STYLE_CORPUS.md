## Recurrent rhetorical structure

Across this corpus, abstracts usually establish importance, identify one unresolved point, state the study action, report quantitative or mechanistic results, and end with a bounded implication. Introductions progress from system-level biology to the precise unresolved mechanism and finish with a synopsis of the study's results. Result headings state findings, and result paragraphs move from question to evidence to local interpretation. Methods are placed after Discussion in the dominant recent layout. Captions are independently interpretable and carry experimental definitions, sample sizes, visual encodings and statistics. Discussions lead with the main conclusion, connect it to adjacent mechanisms, and give concrete limitations rather than a generic closing summary.

The rewritten manuscript follows this structure while avoiding direct reuse of source wording.
