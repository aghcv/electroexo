# Communications Biology figure audit

The draft reduces the main article from more than ten figures plus large tables to five figures. Layer-specific simulations, residual maps, conditional variance analysis, local-information diagnostics and decision aids are now Supplementary Figures 1--12. The extended dosimetry schematic is Supplementary Figure 13.

Before submission, regenerate the figures with the following shared settings:

- Arial or Helvetica throughout;
- approximately 8 pt lettering at final displayed size;
- line widths of at least 1 pt at final size;
- bold lower-case panel labels (`a`, `b`, ...), replacing legacy upper-case labels;
- white backgrounds and RGB colour;
- colour-blind-accessible palette; avoid red--green and rainbow scales;
- at least 300 dpi for raster content, with vector PDF or SVG preferred for plots;
- individual observations shown wherever they exist;
- no decorative boxes or embedded explanatory paragraphs that belong in captions;
- axes should identify quantity and unit, and should not be truncated to amplify differences.

The copied files preserve current numerical content for drafting. They have not been raster-edited, because changing labels directly in rendered images would compromise reproducibility. Their generating scripts should be updated and rerun from the archived source data.
