# Submission gaps requiring confirmation

These items cannot be inferred safely from the available manuscript and data exports.

- corresponding-author email;
- author order, contribution roles and funding;
- CD4-positive producer-cell source, phenotype and culture format;
- electrode geometry and separation;
- delivered electric field rather than generator-voltage labels;
- pulse width, waveform, repetition rate and temperature control for each experiment;
- culture volume and viable-cell counts at every harvest;
- harvest, sampling and medium-replacement protocol;
- dilution and particle-recovery factors;
- Exoid instrument model, settings, calibration, detection limits and quality-control rules;
- relationship between cell-containing sham and `sham media` files;
- whether repeated histograms are biological, technical or nested replicates;
- randomization, blinding and exclusion procedures;
- raw-data availability and permission to deposit;
- public code/data repository identifiers.

Until resolved, the manuscript treats voltage values as source labels, repeated measurements descriptively, the 5-mL culture volume as provisional, and all fitted quantities as in-sample effective parameters.
