# Communications Biology manuscript template

This is an authoring template for an initial or revised primary-research submission to *Communications Biology*. It intentionally uses a readable one-column A4 layout; the journal does not require authors to imitate its published two-column typesetting.

Compile `main.tex` to produce the article PDF. Compile `supplementary/supplementary_information.tex` separately because the Supplementary Information must be a self-contained PDF.

Key limits and conventions:

- title: 15 words or fewer;
- abstract: 150 words or fewer, accessible to non-specialists and without citations;
- Introduction + Results + Discussion: approximately 5,000 words;
- main display items: no more than 10, with approximately five for a 2,500-word paper;
- preferred section order: Introduction, Results, Discussion, Methods;
- figures: RGB, at least 300 dpi, prepared near final size; use Arial or Helvetica at approximately 8 pt and lines at least 1 pt wide;
- use colour-blind-accessible palettes and avoid red--green or rainbow encodings;
- show individual data points rather than summary-only bars;
- include a `Statistics and reproducibility` subsection;
- provide mandatory Data availability and, for central custom code, Code availability statements;
- place all Supplementary Information in one independently understandable PDF;
- number references in order of appearance and do not use footnotes.

The journal states that strict production formatting is not required at initial submission. Verify requirements against the current official guidance before submission.
