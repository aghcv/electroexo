# Figure specifications

Prepare each figure at the size expected in the submitted or final A4-oriented article.

- Use Arial or Helvetica consistently across every figure.
- Use approximately 8 pt lettering at final size.
- Use lines no thinner than 1 pt at final size.
- Supply RGB files at 300 dpi or greater.
- Prefer editable vector files (`.pdf`, `.svg`, `.eps`, or `.ai`) for plots and diagrams.
- Use a white background and accessible colours with comparable visibility.
- Avoid red--green contrasts, rainbow colour maps, decorative effects and truncated axes that exaggerate differences.
- Use bold lower-case panel labels (`a`, `b`, ...).
- Use scale bars rather than magnification factors.
- Show distributions or individual observations rather than summary-only bar charts.
- Give each caption a short whole-figure title followed by panel-specific descriptions, sample sizes, statistics and abbreviation definitions.
